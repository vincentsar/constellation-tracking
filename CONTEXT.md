# Systemic constellation sessions

A constellation session contains spatial representations and a record of what people say during the exercise.

## Language

**Piece**:
A visual marker for a role assignment on a particular slide.

**Representative**:
The human participant acting for the person or concept represented by a piece.
_Avoid_: Representation, editor

**Representation**:
The person or concept a piece stands for; this may remain unnamed until disclosed during the session.
_Avoid_: Representative

**Role assignment**:
A particular association of a representative with a representation, which may span several slides. The same representative may have distinct assignments, such as Alice as Mother and Alice as Fear.

**Master list**:
The session-wide list of role assignments, including the slides on which each appears.

**Transcript**:
The chronological record of statements and questions in the session, with entries attributed to speakers.

**Assignment mention**:
A linked reference to a role assignment within transcript text, inserted using `@`. It is distinct from the assignment speaking an entry.

**Slide**:
An editable stage of a constellation session grouping a spatial arrangement with its conversations.

**Facing**:
The direction toward which a piece is oriented in the constellation.
